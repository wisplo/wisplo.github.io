Frequently asked questions

Which chat apps does Chatwand work with?
LinkedIn, WhatsApp Web, Slack, X (Twitter), Instagram, Messenger, Facebook, YouTube, Telegram, Discord, Microsoft Teams, HubSpot, Google Chat, Reddit, Twitch, Tawk.to and Chatwoot - the web versions you open in your browser.
Do you store my messages or quick replies on a server?
No. Chatwand has no server. Your quick replies and settings are stored locally in your browser. The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to upgrade to Pro, which is handled by our payment processor. See our Privacy Policy.
How does the Pro upgrade work?
Pro is a subscription billed monthly ($4.99) or yearly ($39). Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, who collect and remit any applicable tax. After purchase you receive a license key to activate Pro on up to 5 devices. You can cancel anytime.
Can I get a refund?
Yes - see our Refund & Cancellation Policy. EU/EEA customers also have statutory withdrawal rights.
