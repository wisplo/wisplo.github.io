How to delete all your Google Maps reviews at once

Old reviews you'd rather not have under your name? Here's what Google lets you do, and the faster way when there are dozens of them.

Updated October 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: Google Maps has no "delete all reviews" button. You can delete reviews one at a time from Your contributions on a computer or in the app. To delete many at once, a browser extension such as Mapwand can work through the list for you, clicking the same delete buttons you would.

Before you delete anything

  • It's permanent. A deleted review can't be restored, and any photos you attached to it are deleted too.
  • Local Guides points. If you're a Local Guide, you'll likely lose the points those reviews earned.
  • Keep a copy if you want one. Google Takeout can export your reviews - select "Maps (your places)".
  • Give it a moment. It can take a little while for a deleted review to disappear from a place's page everywhere.

Method 1: delete reviews one by one

Fine for a handful of reviews. Google moves these menus around now and then, so the labels may differ slightly.

On a computer

  1. Open Google Maps and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (☰) at the top left, or click your profile picture, and choose Your contributions. You can also go straight to google.com/maps/contrib.
  3. Select the Reviews tab.
  4. Next to a review, click More (the three dots) and choose Delete review.
  5. Confirm, then repeat for the next one.

On a phone (Android or iPhone)

  1. Open the Google Maps app and tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Your profile (or Your contributions) and go to your reviews.
  3. Tap More (the three dots) next to a review, then Delete review, and confirm.

Each review takes about 10 to 15 seconds of menu-clicking. With 200 reviews, that's well over half an hour.

Common questions

Can I delete a review someone else wrote?
No - only the account that wrote a review can delete it, and that includes Mapwand, which only works on your own reviews. If you own a business and a review breaks Google's content policies, you can report it from your Google Business Profile.
Can I get a deleted review back?
No. Google has no undo for deleted reviews, so export a copy with Google Takeout first if you might want them later.
Is bulk deleting safe for my Google account?
You're only removing your own content, the same way you would by hand. Mapwand adds randomised pauses between deletions and stops if Google shows a verification step. Any automated activity on Google services carries some risk, though, so if you have hundreds of reviews, splitting them across a few sittings is the gentlest approach.
Can I delete just some of my reviews?
By hand, yes - pick any review and delete it. Mapwand currently works from the top of your list down, and you can stop it at any point.
Can I delete my Google Maps photos or edits too?
Yes, by hand, from the Photos and Edits tabs of Your contributions. Mapwand deletes reviews only (including photos attached to a review); it doesn't handle stand-alone photos or edits yet.
Does this work on my phone?
The manual method does. Mapwand is a desktop browser extension for Chrome (and Chromium-based browsers such as Edge) on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS.
