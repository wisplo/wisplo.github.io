02 · How we build

What Wisplo stands for

The same three rules run through everything we make.

I

Private by design

Our tools store your data on your own device, using Chrome's built-in storage. No servers collecting your content, no tracking, no selling data.

II

Focused, not bloated

Each product does one job well. No dashboards to learn, no settings you'll never touch. Install it and get on with your day.

III

Fair & transparent

Clear pricing, easy cancellation, and a real person answering support email. Payments are handled by a trusted merchant of record.
