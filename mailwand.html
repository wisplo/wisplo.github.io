Built for people who send the same kinds of messages every day - support, sales, recruiting, freelancing, admin.

Reusable templates

Save the emails you send over and over. Insert them instantly from a floating button, a keyboard shortcut, or by typing /your-shortcut while you write.

✍️

Rich text & signatures

Bold, italics, lists, links, colors and highlights. Keep multiple sign-offs and drop the right signature in with a single click.

📽

Merge fields

Add {{name}}, {{company}} and more. Mailwand asks you to fill them in before inserting, so every email feels personal.

⏰

Follow-up reminders

Set a nudge for later today, tomorrow, in a few days, or a custom time. Mailwand chimes, shows a notification, and takes you back to the thread.

📁

Folders & search

Organize templates into folders so the right reply is always a click away, even when you have dozens of them.

🔒

Private by design

No servers and no tracking. Your templates and reminders are stored locally on your device. Mailwand never sends your emails anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Which email services does Mailwand work with?
Gmail, Outlook.com / Hotmail, Outlook on Office 365, and Yahoo Mail - the web versions you open in your browser.
Do you store my emails or templates on a server?
No. Mailwand has no server. Your templates, signatures and reminders are stored locally in your browser. The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to upgrade to Pro, which is handled by our payment processor. See our Privacy Policy.
How does the Pro upgrade work?
Pro is a subscription billed monthly ($4.99) or yearly ($39). Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, who collect and remit any applicable tax. After purchase you receive a license key to activate Pro on your device. You can cancel anytime.
