01

Find your key

Look for an email titled "Your Mapwand Pro license key" from Dodo Payments, our payment provider. It usually arrives within a few minutes - check Spam or Promotions too.

02

Open Mapwand

Click the Mapwand icon in your Chrome toolbar, then I already have a license key → under the upgrade button.

03

Paste & activate

Paste the key and click Activate. The badge switches to ✓ Premium: unlimited deletions, speed control and your deletion history are unlocked.

If something's not right

No email after 15 minutes
Check Spam and Promotions first. Still nothing? Email wisplo.support@gmail.com from the address you paid with, and we'll send your key manually.
"License key is not valid"
Copy the whole key from the email, with no spaces before or after it, and try again. If it still fails, email us the key and we'll check it with Dodo Payments.
I can't see the Mapwand icon
Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome and pin Mapwand. If it isn't listed there, the extension isn't installed in this browser yet - get it from the Chrome Web Store.
New computer or reinstalled Chrome
Your key is stored only in your browser, so after reinstalling just paste the same key from your email again.
