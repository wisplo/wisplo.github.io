Mapwand is installed

Three steps to clean up your Google Maps reviews.

Mapwand works inside your own Google Maps tab, on the account you're already signed in to. Nothing to set up, no account to create.

01

Pin Mapwand

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Mapwand. Its teal map-pin icon now stays in your toolbar.

02

Open your contributions

Make sure you're signed in to Google, then open your Google Maps contributions page. (Mapwand can also open it for you in step 3.)

Open my contributions
03

Delete

Click the Mapwand icon, tick I understand, click Delete my reviews and confirm. You can pause, stop or close the popup at any time.

Good to know before you start

  • Deletions are permanent. Google has no undo. Each review is deleted entirely, including any photos you attached to it.
  • Want a copy first? Google Takeout can export your reviews: choose "Maps (your places)" at takeout.google.com.
  • Free plan: 5 deletions in total. Pro is a one-time $4.99 for unlimited deletions, three speeds and a deletion history. No subscription.
  • Already bought Pro? In the Mapwand popup, click "I already have a license key" and paste the key from your email. More help with activation.
  • Lots of reviews? Splitting a few hundred deletions across a couple of sittings is the gentlest approach for your account.
  • Something not working? Email wisplo.support@gmail.com - Google changes its pages now and then, and a quick note helps us fix Mapwand fast.
