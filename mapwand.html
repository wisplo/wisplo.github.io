A note on responsible use

Mapwand only works on your own Google account, and only deletes contributions you own.

What Mapwand does & doesn't do

Mapwand uses the same Google Maps web interface you'd use yourself, clicking the same delete buttons, with built-in randomised pauses. It only deletes contributions on the Google account currently signed into your browser. It can't access anyone else's reviews. It pauses immediately if Google shows a CAPTCHA. You can stop a run at any moment, and every action is logged locally so you can see exactly what was removed.

Frequently asked questions

If anything's missing here, just email us.

Is this allowed? Will my Google account get in trouble?
Mapwand only deletes contributions you own, using the same buttons you'd click yourself. It never logs into your account and never scrapes data. The randomised, human-like pauses and CAPTCHA-aware behavior are designed specifically to stay within reasonable automation behavior. That said, all bulk actions on Google services carry some risk - if you have hundreds of items to remove, splitting them across a few sittings is the safest route.
Does Mapwand store or send my data anywhere?
No. Mapwand has no server. Your deletion history, settings and license key are stored locally in your browser. The only network call we ever make is to Dodo Payments to validate your Pro license, nothing else leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy.
