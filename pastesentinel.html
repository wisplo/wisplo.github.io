Frequently asked questions

Which AI chat sites does PasteSentinel work with?
ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), Perplexity (perplexity.ai), Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com), Mistral's Le Chat (chat.mistral.ai), Poe (poe.com) and DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com) - the web versions you open in your browser.
Does PasteSentinel store or send my data anywhere?
No. PasteSentinel has no server. The text you paste is scanned entirely in memory, in your browser, and discarded the instant the check completes - it's never written to storage or sent anywhere, even when a secret is found. Only your on/off setting, your own ignore-list entries, and a simple detection counter are saved, locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
What does it actually detect?
Dozens of named formats - AWS, GitHub, Slack, Stripe, OpenAI-style, Anthropic, Google, Twilio, SendGrid, npm, GitLab, Discord and more - plus private key files (RSA, EC, OpenSSH, DSA, PKCS#8, encrypted PEM, PGP) and database connection strings with embedded passwords. Anything that doesn't match a known format but still looks like a strong, random secret is caught by a local entropy check. Your SSH .pub file is deliberately left alone, since that's meant to be shared.
Will it flag things that aren't secrets?
Rarely, but any scanner like this can have false positives on unusual-looking text. If PasteSentinel flags something you use on purpose, like a placeholder or test key, add it to your ignore list once from the extension's settings and it won't be flagged again.
