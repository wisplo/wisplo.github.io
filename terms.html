1. The Services

Wisplo builds small, private browser extensions:

Mailwand

Lets you save and insert reusable email templates and signatures, use merge fields and shortcuts, and set follow-up reminders within supported webmail services (Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail, and Yahoo Mail).

Mapwand

Lets you bulk-delete your own Google Maps reviews from your "Your contributions" page, with confirmation and human-like timing.

Chatwand

Lets you save and insert reusable quick replies and canned responses, with optional fill-in fields, within supported chat, social and support apps.

PasteSentinel

Scans text you paste into supported AI chat sites (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Mistral's Le Chat, Poe and DeepSeek) for patterns that look like an API key, token, password or other secret, and warns you before the paste completes. Currently in Chrome Web Store review.

Our tools run locally in your browser; we do not operate a server that stores your content. See our Privacy Policy for details on what each extension handles.
